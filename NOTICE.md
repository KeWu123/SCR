# Rights and provenance

Original research code and results are provided for inspection and reproduction
of the accompanying paper. No additional license grant is made for original
research material.

The FAISS source archive and instrumentation-derived material retain the
upstream MIT license and copyright notices under `experiments/native_trace/`.
Scientific Python dependencies are installed separately and retain their own
licenses. No dependency runtime, local credentials or operating-system fonts
are redistributed.

`PROVENANCE.json` records the historical and released identities of selected
files. Relocation, machine-path redaction, dependency discovery and checker
portability can change file bytes. Historical hashes are identifiers of the
retained research snapshot; they are not evidence that a later edited file
executed an earlier experiment. Numerical measurements are retained as recorded.
Additional native-trace and interval provenance is described in their READMEs.
